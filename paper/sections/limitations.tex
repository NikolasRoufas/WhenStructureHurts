\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Two 7B readers, one prompt family.} All results come from Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, with Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 as an exploratory second reader on the same questions, both with greedy decoding. Two readers cannot establish behaviour of model families in general, and larger or differently trained models may treat asserted contradictions differently. Most effects act through abstention under a prompt that offers \emph{insufficient evidence}; without that option the differences shrink but wrong answers rise.

\paragraph{Asserted, not verified, falsity.} The confirmed result concerns showing NLI-predicted contradiction lines. That most of them are false rests on automatic triage; the blinded two-annotator package is prepared but not labelled, so we report no human-verified precision, and we did not run the intervention that removes only verified false contradictions. Every derived dependency edge is derived by rule, and a substantial fraction has a fallback anchor; no relation is human-verified, and the component-attribution experiment was not run.

\paragraph{Sentence units, not propositions.} Our fact units are dataset sentences, some of which depend on their passage, not LLM-extracted atomic propositions. The results concern sentence-item presentation, not the end-to-end effect of claim extraction, which we observed only at pilot scale with unequal budgets.

\paragraph{Gold evidence and retrieval.} The confirmatory tests and controls use gold supporting passages. The retrieved-evidence experiment uses 500 questions per dataset, its MuSiQue sample is almost entirely 2-hop, and annotation-derived structure rarely survives top-4 retrieval, so correct structure on incomplete evidence remains untested.

\paragraph{Exploratory analyses.} The fixed-order controls, the prompt variants, the second reader and the retrieved-evidence experiment were designed after the first confirmatory result and are not multiplicity-corrected; only the contradiction contrast was tested prospectively. A planned regression of the contradiction effect on line counts was abandoned because its predictors are collinear.

\paragraph{Metric and pilot scale.} Exact match is strict, especially for a reader that answers in sentences; we therefore also report a lenient match fixed before that reader's run. Protocol~B, Experiment~0 and the perturbations were run only at pilot scale, and the pilot overestimated the first confirmed effect by a factor of four.

\paragraph{Compute.} All runs used one 32\,GB GPU with batch size~1, about 20 GPU-hours in total.
