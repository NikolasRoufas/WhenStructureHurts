\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{One reader, one prompt.} All generations come from Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct with greedy decoding. The confirmed effects act mainly through abstention under a prompt that offers \emph{insufficient evidence}, and they largely vanish without that option. Other model families, larger models, or other instructions may behave differently. The confirmatory sample is disjoint from the pilot, but it is not an independent replication with a different reader, dataset or team. A second-reader replication is prepared but was not run.

\paragraph{Derived, not verified, structure.} Every dependency edge is derived from dataset annotations by rule, and for a substantial fraction of questions the claim-level endpoint is a fallback guess. No relation in this study is human-verified. We did not run the component-attribution experiment, which requires verified structure, and we report no relation precision or recall. The predicted contradictions are judged false only by automatic triage. Two blinded annotation packages are prepared but not completed.

\paragraph{Sentence units, not propositions.} Our fact units are dataset sentences. Some depend on their passage to be interpreted, and they are not LLM-extracted atomic propositions. Our results concern sentence-item presentation, not the end-to-end effect of claim extraction, which we observed only at pilot scale with unequal budgets.

\paragraph{Gold evidence and retrieval.} The confirmatory test and the controls use gold supporting passages. The retrieved-evidence replication uses 500 questions per dataset; its MuSiQue sample is almost entirely 2-hop, because earlier samples exhausted the longer questions. Annotation-derived structure rarely survives top-4 retrieval, so correct structure on incomplete evidence remains untested.

\paragraph{Exploratory analyses.} The fixed-order controls, the prompt factor and the retrieved-evidence experiment were designed after the confirmatory result. They were specified before they were run, but they are not multiplicity-corrected and are not confirmations. The controls run on the confirmatory questions. A planned regression of the contradiction effect on line counts was abandoned because its predictors are collinear.

\paragraph{Metric and pilot scale.} Exact match is strict; a diacritic-folding check changed few records and no conclusion. Protocol~B, Experiment~0 and the perturbations were run only on the 100-question-per-dataset pilot. The pilot overestimated the one effect we later confirmed by a factor of four.

\paragraph{Compute.} All runs used one 32\,GB GPU with batch size~1, about 12 GPU-hours in total.
