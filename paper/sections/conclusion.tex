\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Holding the evidence fixed, we found no detectable effect of graph-derived order or derived relation lines for Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct on 4{,}976 held-out questions, and a small, abstention-driven cost of sentence items on 2WikiMultiHopQA. Exploratory fixed-order controls located where structure hurts: predicted contradiction relations cost about three exact-match points by making the reader abstain, an effect that replicated with real retrieval and largely vanished when abstention was not offered.
