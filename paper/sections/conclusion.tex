\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

With the evidence fixed, we found no detectable effect of graph-derived order or derived relation lines on 4{,}976 held-out questions, and a small, reader-specific cost of sentence items. What hurt was an asserted contradiction: with facts and order fixed, showing NLI-predicted \texttt{CONTRADICTS} lines lowered exact match by 5--8 points among the affected questions in a pre-specified test on untouched questions, mainly through abstention, and the cost vanished when only the label was changed. Evidence structure should be judged by what its annotations make a reader do.
