\section*{Ethical Considerations}
\label{sec:ethics}

All questions and passages come from public research datasets (MuSiQue, 2WikiMultiHopQA, HotpotQA, HoVer, FEVER) and are used under their licences for research; we release no new text data. No human subjects were involved in the reported results. The blinded annotation packages we prepared will, if used, require informed, paid annotators and are described in the repository. All models are openly licensed and were run locally on one GPU; the full study used about 20 GPU-hours. Our results concern how a reader responds to evidence formats, including a tendency to abstain when shown asserted conflicts; they do not make systems more capable of harm, but they do suggest that automatically extracted relations can silently reduce the answers a deployed system gives. We used an AI assistant extensively for code, experiment execution and drafting; the scope of this use is disclosed in the Responsible NLP checklist, and the authors are responsible for all content.
